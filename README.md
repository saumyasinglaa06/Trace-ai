 Technical Root-cause Analysis & Correlation Engine

TRACE is an incident intelligence system that turns customer support complaints into actionable engineering investigations.

Instead of treating every customer complaint as a separate ticket, TRACE identifies similar complaints, groups them into an incident, analyzes the pattern, and produces a probable root cause with recommended engineering actions.


\The Problem

During technical failures, support teams may receive hundreds of complaints such as:

- "Payment succeeded but my order is missing."
- "I was charged but checkout failed."
- "My bank says the payment was successful, but there is no order."

These may look like separate customer issues, but they can actually be symptoms of the same engineering failure.

TRACE connects the customer side of the problem with the engineering side.
